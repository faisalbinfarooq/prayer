Prayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Prayer is an offline prayer-times app. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

What the app stores, and where

All settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage (Jetpack DataStore):

  • the selected location: city name, latitude/longitude and time zone (a built-in city, or your GPS position if you choose "Use my location");
  • the calculation method, Asr madhab, per-prayer alarm switches and the "Remind me before" setting;
  • theme and language;
  • the Tasbeeh counter (preset, count, target, rounds, total);
  • your missed-prayer (qada) counts, how many you have made up, and when they last changed;
  • your prayer record: for each day, which prayers you marked as prayed (in congregation or early), prayed, or missed;
  • whether first-run setup is finished.

Prayer times, the Hijri date, the Qibla bearing and the compass are calculated on the device.

Location

Location is optional. If you tap "Use my location", the app asks for location permission and reads your current position once from Android. It is used on the device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction and is saved only in the settings above. The app does not track your location in the background.

To name that position, the app uses its own built-in list of cities: it shows the nearest one within 50 km, or the coordinates otherwise. No reverse-geocoding or other online service is used, so your position never leaves the device.

Network

The app does not declare the internet permission and makes no network requests. "Find nearby mosques" hands a generic "mosque" map search to your maps app or browser; it does not include your coordinates. What that app does is governed by its own privacy policy.

Android backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's settings (including the saved location), your missed-prayer counts and your prayer record in your device backup and restore them on a new phone. This backup is handled by Android and your backup provider (for example Google); the developer cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Syncing with another phone

The Qada screen can show your missed-prayer counts as a QR code or a short text code. They go to another phone only when you show that code or send it yourself (for example by a messaging app you choose); the app sends nothing over the internet. Scanning uses the Google Play services code scanner, which runs on your phone and gives the app only the scanned text; the app has no camera permission.

Permissions

  • Location (precise/approximate): only when you choose GPS location, as described above.
  • Notifications: prayer alarm and reminder notifications.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms), full-screen notifications, foreground service (media playback), vibrate, run at startup: to ring the prayer alarm on time, show it over the lock screen, play the adhan or alarm sound, give tasbeeh feedback, and re-arm the alarm after a restart.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Prayer > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstall the app. Data in an Android backup is managed in your backup provider's settings.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
